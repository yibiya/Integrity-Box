# The Crow I Couldn't Help

Today I saw a crow nearby a graveyard.

I noticed something was wrong with it because it wasn't flying. It was kind of jumping and dragging one side, and then I saw that one of its wings was probably broken.

I went towards it and it immediately started trying to get away.

I don't even know why I started chasing it. I think I just wanted to catch it because I thought maybe I could do something.

It kept jumping ahead and I kept following it.

Then it went into the bushes.

I stood there for a bit trying to see where it went. I moved some of the leaves and then it came out a little further inside and just stopped.

It looked at me.

I remember that part very clearly.

**It was scared of me**

I slowly put my hand near it and touched its feathers. I could have probably grabbed it then but I just couldn't. I kept thinking what if I hurt its wing more or what if it bites me. I don't know.

So I left it.

I feel a little bad about that now.

The thing I remember most is its beak, which sounds stupid because it's just a crow, but it was really shiny and straight. I don't remember crows looking like that. When I was in school and had to draw a crow I'd just draw that stupid curved triangle beak and a black body. I never actually looked at one properly.

This one looked different when I was standing so close to it.

**I've been thinking about it since I came home.**

I keep wondering where it went after I left.

Maybe it's still somewhere in those bushes. Maybe it managed to get somewhere safer. Maybe someone else found it and actually picked it up.

I really hope somebody did.

I'm going there again tomorrow.

If I find it, I'll try to help this time.

I just hope I find it.
